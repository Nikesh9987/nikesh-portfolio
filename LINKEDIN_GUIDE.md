# LinkedIn Portfolio Integration Guide

> Turn your portfolio URL into a powerful lead generation and recruiter magnet on LinkedIn.

---

## Overview

Your portfolio is only as powerful as its visibility. This guide covers every place on LinkedIn where your portfolio URL should appear — and how to write copy that makes recruiters and clients actually click.

---

## 1. Profile URL — Add to "Website" field

**Where:** Profile → **Contact info** (pencil icon) → **Websites**

**Steps:**
1. Go to your LinkedIn profile
2. Click the **pencil icon** on your intro card
3. Scroll down to **Contact info**
4. Under **Websites**, click **Add website**
5. Fill in:
   - **URL:** `https://nikeshmandal.dev`
   - **Type:** Select **Portfolio** (or Other → type "Portfolio")
6. Click **Save**

Your URL now appears on your public profile and in connection requests.

## 4. Featured Section — Pin your portfolio

The **Featured** section appears prominently on your profile — prime real estate.

**Steps:**
1. On your profile, scroll to **Featured** (below About)
2. Click the **+** button → **Add a link**
3. Fill in:
   - **URL:** `https://nikeshmandal.dev`
   - **Title:** `My Developer Portfolio — Projects, Services & Demo Videos`
   - **Description:** `Full stack projects in Java, Spring Boot, React & Python. Live demos, video walkthroughs, and freelance service offerings.`
4. Click **Save**

**Also add your resume:**
1. Click **+** → **Add a media**
2. Upload your `resume.pdf`
3. Title: `Nikesh Mandal — Resume 2024`

## 9. Open To Work settings

1. Click your profile photo → **View Profile**
2. Click **Open to** → **Finding a new job**
3. Fill in:
   - **Job titles:** Full Stack Developer, Java Developer, Software Engineer, Backend Developer
   - **Locations:** Mumbai, Remote, Worldwide
   - **Job types:** Full-time, Contract, Freelance
   - **Start date:** Immediately
4. Choose: **Share with all LinkedIn members** (maximizes visibility)

## 10. LinkedIn profile URL — Customize it

A custom URL looks more professional in email signatures.

**Steps:**
1. Profile → **Edit public profile & URL** (top right)
2. Under **Edit your custom URL**, change to:
   ```
   linkedin.com/in/nikeshmandal
   ```
3. Click **Save**

Now use this clean URL everywhere: emails, resume, GitHub bio, business cards.
